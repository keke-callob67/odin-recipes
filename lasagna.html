Lasagna

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jar tomato pasta sauce
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. In a large skillet, cook ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  3. Add diced onion and minced garlic to the skillet. Cook until the onion is translucent.
  4. Stir in tomato pasta sauce, crushed tomatoes, salt, and Italian seasoning. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a separate bowl, combine ricotta cheese and egg.
  6. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  7. In a baking dish, spread a layer of meat sauce on the bottom. Place a layer of noodles on top.
  8. Spread a layer of the ricotta mixture over the noodles, followed by a layer of mozzarella cheese.
  9. Repeat layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with a layer of meat sauce and mozzarella cheese on top.
  10. Sprinkle grated Parmesan cheese over the top.
  11. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 20 minutes or until cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
  12. Let lasagna rest for 10 minutes before serving. Enjoy!
